Taynnections

A Taylor Swift Connections game by Matteo B.

You are playing Taynnections! Yay! Here are a few tips about how this game works.

The aim of the game is to put the sixteen tiles into four distinct categories by clicking on them. Categories may concern: album, duration, cover, lyrics, variants, music video, genre, writer(s), title, track number, and so on, and so forth.

There are four colors of categories: green, orange, red, and purple, ranging from easy to very hard.

Each grid has a certain level of difficulty, spanning from very easy to very hard.

When you are one tile away from finding the right answer, then the game informs you about it.

Hints: You may ask for hints. However, hints are not available for very easy grids. Additionally, the maximum number of hint requests is 1, 2, and 3 for easy and medium, hard, and very hard grids, respectively. Hints may have various aspects: lyrics, in italic and slightly modified, or crossword-like sentences. If a lyric does not have a specific word, then this word is the hint. For example, with Fever dream high in the night, the hint is the word quiet, since it is missing from the lyric.

Please note that hints may not be available for your device since it is too small. Do consider using a bigger screen.

Additional info: If multiple variants of a song exist but no further indication is given, then consider it to be irrelevant or the default version (i.e. Taylor's Version, for example). Tip: If a further indication is given, then it may have a certain importance to the category.

The webmaster may be contacted for any review, correction, question, grid proposal or complaint about the game.

And, last but not least, have fun playing Taynnections!
